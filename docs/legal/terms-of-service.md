## 2. What the Service is

LEX Practice is software for managing a legal practice: matters, tasks, a cause
list, documents, client communication and reporting. It is a tool for
practitioners. **It is not a law firm, it does not provide legal advice, and it
does not exercise professional judgement on your behalf.**

Two things follow from that, and they matter:

- **Conflict screening is an aid, not a clearance.** The Service compares the
  parties you enter against records already in your chamber and reports possible
  matches. It cannot know about matters outside the system, it will miss
  conflicts recorded under different names, and it will raise matches that are
  not conflicts. The professional decision remains entirely yours.
- **Deadline reminders are a convenience, not a diary system.** You remain
  responsible for every limitation period, filing date and listing.
